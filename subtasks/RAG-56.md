# RAG-56 — Local transformers.js embeddings adapter (in-process, free, no key)

> Parent: second `EmbeddingProvider` impl, env-selected, to prove the swap seam and give the free/self-hosted story Voyage can't. Concrete choice (vs. the parent's "Ollama/OpenAI-compatible" hint): **in-process transformers.js** (`@huggingface/transformers`) — no separate server, no key, no rate limit. Source: TDD §2.1, PRD FR-2, rules `evals.md` (retrieval-affecting) + `coding-standards.md` (adapter seam). Baseline to beat/hold: **voyage-4-lite → hit-rate 10/10, avg precision@5 0.43, abstain-rate 4/6, MIN_SCORE=0.3** (RAG-57).
